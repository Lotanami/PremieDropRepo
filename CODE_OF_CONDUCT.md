# Code of Conduct

PremieDrop is an open-source project for video editors and developers. The
goal is to make the project useful, welcoming, and easy to improve.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient with other contributors.
- Give clear, specific feedback.
- Assume good intent when someone is learning or asking basic questions.
- Keep criticism focused on the code, design, workflow, or documentation.
- Help keep issues and pull requests understandable for future contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Dismissing contributors because of experience level.
- Posting private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

## Reporting Problems

If something happens that makes the project feel unsafe or hostile, contact the
repo owner through GitHub. If the problem is public, you can also flag the
comment or issue through GitHub's reporting tools.

Reports will be handled as fairly and privately as possible. Depending on the
situation, action may include a warning, deleting comments, locking threads, or
blocking users from the project.
